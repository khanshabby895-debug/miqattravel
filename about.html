About Miqat Travels

Helping You Prepare for a Meaningful Hajj & Umrah Journey

At Miqat Travels, we understand that Hajj and Umrah are more than journeys. They are deeply meaningful experiences, and we are here to help make your travel arrangements simple, organised and comfortable.

From the United Kingdom, we help pilgrims explore suitable Hajj and Umrah travel options, including flights, accommodation and travel arrangements. Our aim is to provide clear information and dedicated assistance from the initial enquiry through your journey planning.

Every pilgrim and family has different needs. That is why we focus on understanding your requirements and helping you choose a package that fits your preferred travel dates, duration, accommodation and budget.
